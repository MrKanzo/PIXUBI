# PIXUBI 0.28.4 — Iskryk baby
A 768×768 transparent illustrated atlas with 16 cells. Walk (0–3), seated (4), airborne (5), sniff (6), curled sleep (7), and eight front expressions (8–15). The shared world and developer sandbox choose actual complete sprite frames; no stretched limbs. Iskryk uses a discrete jump sequence during expeditions and normal gait at home. Search hiding uses the sniff pose. Both sexes use the baby artwork and existing sex/gene markers. Other growth stages and species retain their previous sprites. The sniff and sleep images are single poses; a fully independent tail/rig and additional stages remain future artwork.
