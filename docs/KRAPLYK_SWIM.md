# PIXUBI 0.28.6 — Kraplyk swimming prototype

Four pixel-aligned swimming frames use the original baby sprite. Only distal gill and tail pixels move; the central face and torso retain their original artwork, including native blink frames. The same animation is used during exploration and in the developer sandbox. An opaque foreground water lip masks the lower body, and the ground shadow is removed during swimming. No pet statistics, evolution thresholds, save format or approved Lisyk/Iskryk art are changed.

This is a targeted native-pixel swimming pass, not a complete new illustrated atlas. Walking, sleep and other expressions still use existing art until dedicated frames are drawn. Verify the swim and dive in the sandbox on-device before approving the visual result.
