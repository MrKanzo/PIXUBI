# Lisyk baby art v1

The approved pixel-art sheet is bundled as lisyk_baby_pixel_poses.png (768x384, eight 192px cells). The first row is a four-frame gait; the second is seated, standing, sniffing and curled asleep. The sprite player uses the complete cells, never moves a cut-out limb. All cells share a planted baseline. The atlas is used only for baby Lisyk, including both sexes and the existing genetic accent convention. Other species and stages retain their original art. Per-pose equipment guides are in LisykBabyArtwork.sockets; these are preliminary coordinates, not yet a fully separated skeleton or an equipped-item UI. Standing, sniff and sleep are single poses; tail motion is part of the walking drawings, not a separate idle wag clip. Native facial animation is available only on the seated pose.
