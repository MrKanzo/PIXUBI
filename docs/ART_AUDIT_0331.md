# PIXUBI 0.33.1 — audit and animation matrix

The active renderer uses six original pose packs (five babies and young Lisyk) and fourteen temporary 64px sheets for other forms. The existing older reference atlas (192px) has distinct species anatomy but is not the active full-pose source. Do not confuse the temporary sheets with final art approval.

| Species | Keep from original | Art work still required |
|---|---|---|
| Lisyk | Baby and juvenile pose packs | Distinct adult/ultra anatomy and equipment; preserve the established fox head and tail |
| Iskryk | Striped tiger cub and lightning tail | Stripes, cat muzzle and lightning tail in all older forms; digging/sniffing loop needs more poses |
| Kraplyk | Axolotl head, gills, swimming tail | No fox ears or paws on older forms; dedicated lying sleep pose and full swim cycle |
| Pishchanyk | Large fennec ears, goggles, digging | Bigger ears, desert accessories and real digging cycle at older ages; dedicated baby sleep |
| Khmaryk | Owl face, feathered wings, four flight poses | Older forms must have owl anatomy and clear wingbeats; dedicated baby sleep |

Both sexes currently derive a small silhouette and palette variant from the same base artwork, not forty separately illustrated sheets.

This revision unifies capability selection, side mirroring, stage scale and scene movement. The developer animation matrix previews the same renderer and clock as the game, for each selected species, stage, sex and gene. Unsupported species actions are marked instead of showing an unrelated pose. No original PNGs are removed or replaced.

Review all 20 forms × 2 sexes before final art approval. Still pending: frame-by-frame artist review of older pose sheets, separate sex-specific art and device validation. The expedition scene remains separately available in the developer menu.
